# Finite-droplet static stability contract (P1/I2)

This contract defines the live calculation in
`verification/nvg_droplet_stability_audit.py`.  It is a zero-temperature,
symmetric, Coulomb-free Thomas--Fermi second-variation calculation around the
accepted `N=208` spherical W8 backgrounds at `y*=.90` and `.93`.  Its
independent-evidence weight is zero.  The inverse W8 calibration inputs and
the finite-droplet producer remain the source of the background; this surface
does not identify the configurations with real nuclei.

## Variables and Hessian

All radial quantities use

\[
x=W_0r/(\hbar c),\qquad y=W/W_0,\qquad a=A/W_0,
\qquad n=n_{\rm nat}/W_0^3,
\]

with `q=momega/W0`, `gs=M/W0`, and a real spherical harmonic normalized by
`integral |Y_lm|^2 dOmega=1`.  For `delta n=uY_lm` and `delta y=vY_lm`,

\[
\frac{Q_l}{W_0}=\int x^2\left[A u^2+2Buv+C v^2+(v')^2+
\frac{l(l+1)}{x^2}v^2\right]dx+\langle s,K_l^{-1}s\rangle_{x^2dx},
\]

where

\[
A=F_{nn}=\frac{k^2}{3ne},\quad B=F_{ny}=\frac{g_s^2y}{e},\quad
C=F_{yy}+U_{yy}-q^2a^2,
\]

\[
F_{yy}=\frac{g_s^2d}{2\pi^2}\int_0^k
\frac{p^4\,dp}{[p^2+(g_sy)^2]^{3/2}},qquad
s=gu-2q^2ya v,
\]

and

\[
K_l=-x^{-2}\partial_x(x^2\partial_x)+l(l+1)x^{-2}+q^2y^2.
\]

The nonlocal term is positive and has a plus sign because the Gauss field is
eliminated at its saddle.  The vector-mass and scalar-vector mixing terms are
retained.  `F_nn` is evaluated only on occupied cells; no density floor is
introduced.  Density directions are generated by an actual displacement
potential,

\[
u=-x^{-2}\partial_x(x^2n\psi')+l(l+1)n\psi/x^2,
\]

so the fixed-number condition and the moving Thomas--Fermi edge are retained.
Products containing `F_nn` are integrated to the interpolated `nu=m` edge,
not across the active-mask discontinuity.

For `ell=0`, every sampled density column is measured with an independent
edge-aware integral `|integral x^2 u|/integral x^2|u|` and projected onto the
fixed-number hyperplane when the sampled displacement derivative leaves
quadrature leakage.  The tolerance is `1e-6`; exactly zero columns are
explicitly assigned zero residual.  A failed constraint is a terminal control
failure, not a positive result.

The radial `K_l` discretization is a positive self-adjoint P1 weak operator
with regular-origin and Dirichlet vacuum-tail boundary conditions.  The
generalized eigenproblem uses the positive Gram form
`integral x^2[(u/n0)^2+v^2] dx`; conditioning, absolute whitened residuals in
`Q/W0` units, and rank truncation are reported.  For `ell=1`, the raw matrix
retains the joint translation coefficient vector
`t=(1,0,...;1,0,...)`; the internal spectrum is computed with an explicit
`G`-orthogonal projector `P` spanning `null(t^T G)`.  Exactly one direction is
removed and the measured orthogonality is reported in every trial space; the
raw translation eigenvalue is never relabeled as an internal dipole mode.
Four nested block sizes `6,10,14,18`, intervals `800,1600,3200`, and domains
`24,32 fm` are visible in every sector `l=0,...,6`, together with a tighter
same-domain `24 fm` background solve (`1600` nodes, tolerance `1e-8`).

## Translation and independent checks

For `l=1` the joint translation is explicitly represented by
`u=-n'`, `v=-y'`, `delta a=-a'`.  The audit reports its direct Rayleigh
curvature and the three differentiated identities, while reporting both raw
and translation-projected spectra.

\[
A u+Bv+gw=0,
\]
\[
B u+[-\nabla_1^2+F_{yy}+U_{yy}-q^2a^2]v-2q^2ya w=0,
\]
\[
K_1w=gu-2q^2ya v.
\]

Finite-domain, mesh and BVP residuals remain explicit; a tiny translation
curvature is not set to zero.  The only removed direction is the explicitly
identified joint translation coefficient vector described above; no arbitrary
eigenmode is discarded.

Two independent compact-interior directions (scalar-only and coupled
zero-integral density/scalar) are checked by centered finite differences of
the full radial energy.  The Gauss equation is re-solved at each amplitude,
and amplitudes `.02,.01,.005` are retained.  The corrected upstream
`W8Design.potential_yy` helper is covered by an independent polynomial
derivative regression; only that helper/test correction is authorized by the
sealed revision-2 decision, while `U`, `U_y`, parameters and calibration are
unchanged.

The terminal matrix also exposes the fixed-`N` number drift for every additive
path (limit `1e-10`), positivity of every global Gauss re-solve, the
scalar-only energy-difference agreement, the ell-0 displacement constraints,
nested-basis Rayleigh--Ritz monotonicity with a measured absolute residual
allowance, and the tighter-background contribution to the spectral error.
These are separate guards from the spectral sign labels; missing or failed
background, constraint, operator, Gram, projection, residual, or FD controls
prevent a positive claim.

## Interpretation and interface

`FINITE_BASIS_POSITIVE_ONLY` means that the explored finite basis and
declared refinements have positive, sign-resolved curvatures in the published
norm (raw for non-dipole sectors, translation-projected/internal for `ell=1`);
it is not full stability, an all-radial theorem, or a frequency/growth-rate
calculation.  `UNRESOLVED...` is retained whenever a declared refinement or
Gram-rank control is insufficient.
`UNRESOLVED_FINITE_BASIS_SIGN_OR_CONVERGENCE` preserves a sector whose basis,
sign, rank or refinement margin is insufficient.  A robust negative result,
if present, is an instability witness.  No nonlinear breakup, dynamical RPA,
finite nucleus phenomenology, gravity, Coulomb/asymmetry, pairing, shells or
empirical validation is included.

The CLI is strict JSON and writes nothing:

```text
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 PYTHONPATH=verification python3 -B verification/nvg_droplet_stability_audit.py
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 PYTHONPATH=verification python3 -B -m unittest verification.test_nvg_droplet_stability_audit -v
```
